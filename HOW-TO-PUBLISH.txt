DISCIPLESHIP CLASS: HOW TO PUT THIS ON THE INTERNET
===================================================

This folder is your whole app. Everything is already connected to your database.
You only need to give it a web address. It takes about five minutes and costs nothing.

STEP 1. Publish the folder (pick one)

  Netlify (easiest)
  1. Go to https://app.netlify.com/drop on a computer.
  2. Drag this whole folder onto the page.
  3. After a few seconds you get a link like https://something-random.netlify.app
  4. Optional: in Netlify, open Site configuration, then Change site name, and pick a nicer name.

  Cloudflare Pages (alternative)
  1. Go to https://dash.cloudflare.com, then Workers & Pages, then Create, then Pages, then Upload assets.
  2. Upload this folder and deploy.

STEP 2. Two settings in your database (Supabase)
  Open https://supabase.com/dashboard/project/sbfecjnffugvqwzgnnqn

  a) Authentication, then Sign In / Providers, then Email:
     turn OFF "Confirm email".
     Without this, every new student must tap a link in an email before they can sign in,
     and Supabase only sends a few of those emails per hour.

  b) Authentication, then URL Configuration:
     set "Site URL" to your new link from Step 1.
     This makes the "Forgot password" email open your app.

STEP 3. Start your class
  1. Open your link on your phone.
  2. Tap "I am a teacher" and make your account.
  3. Your dashboard shows a 6-letter CLASS CODE. Tap "Send the invitation" to text it to students.
  4. Students open the same link, tap "I have a class code", and make their own account.

STEP 3b. A ten-minute test before the first class (please do this)
  1. On your phone, make your teacher account.
  2. On a second phone or a private browser window, join as a student with your class code.
  3. As the student: read Lesson 1, tick a task, write a journal entry and tick "Share this with my teacher",
     write an answer under "Talk it over", and add one prayer request shared with the class.
  4. Back on your teacher account, open Today, People and Prayer. You should see the student, the entry, the answer and the request.
  5. If something looks wrong, tell me exactly what the screen said.

STEP 4. Put it on the home screen (it then opens like a normal app)
  iPhone: open the link in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
  Android: open the link in Chrome, tap the menu, then Install app (or Add to Home screen).

WHAT IS NEW IN THIS VERSION
  * Today: a "Not read yet" card with a Send a friendly reminder button. It opens your phone's share sheet
    (Messenger, Viber, text) with the message already written. Each student's page has a message button too.
  * Class tab: Backup (progress and attendance spreadsheets), a co-leader code, and "Make a new class code".
  * Co-leaders: give them the co-leader code. They tap "I am joining as a co-leader" on the sign-in page.
    They see the same class and can run lessons and take attendance.
  * Student page: "Move to another class" (type the other class's code) and "Remove from my class".
    A student with no class sees a Join a class box on their home screen and can type a new code.
  * Students: "I prayed for this" on classmates' requests, with a count the owner can see; answers before class
    (you can read them on the student's page); a Listen button on verses; a celebration after each finished phase;
    Download my journal on the Journal page.

ALSO NEW: ATTENDANCE CHECK-IN AND MEMORY VERSES
  * Check-in: on the Today tab, tap Open check-in when class starts. Each student sees an "I'm here today" button
    on their home screen and taps it. Their name ticks in "Who is here today?" by itself. You can still tap names.
    Check-in closes by itself after four hours, and also when you move the class to the next lesson.
  * Not here this week: names of anyone missing, with a Send a caring message button. Anyone who has missed more than
    one class in a row is pointed out, because a personal call can mean more than a message.
  * People tab: an Attendance record with a row of squares for each student. Students see their own record on Me.
  * Memory verse: each week's verse has a Practice screen with five steps. Step 1 shows the verse, and each harder
    step hides more words, down to first letters only. Tap a blank to peek. A Listen button reads it aloud.
    "I can say it from memory" marks the verse as known. Known verses are listed on the Me tab to practise again.
    Today shows how many students know this week's verse.
  * One update needed: this version needs the two database changes already made on your Supabase project.
    Nothing for you to do there. Just upload this folder to Netlify again (Deploys, then drag the folder in).

ALSO NEW: "POWERED BY NETLIFY" BADGE NO LONGER COVERS THE MENU
  If Netlify shows its small badge over the bottom corner, the app now notices and moves the menu icons up so
  Prayer and Me stay tappable. To remove the badge itself, look in Netlify under Site configuration. On some
  free plans it cannot be turned off, and the app works around it.

ALSO NEW: SEVERAL GROUPS, ONE LOGIN (UP TO 10)
  * Teachers: open the Class tab. "My groups" lists every group you lead with its material, lesson and student count.
    Tap Add a group, give it a name, choose Disciple or OHB, and tap Create group. Each group has its own class code,
    students, current lesson, attendance and material, so one group can be on Disciple Lesson 10 while another
    is on OHB Lesson 3.
  * Once you lead two or more groups, a Group box appears at the top of every teacher page. Pick a group there to
    switch. Students never see your other groups.
  * Delete this group (Class tab, only for the person who made it) removes the group, its attendance and its
    messages. Its students stay in the app without a class and can join another group with a code.
    You always keep at least one group.
  * A co-leader joins one group with its co-leader code. If the person who made that group deletes it, the
    co-leader is offered a new group of their own.
  * One update needed: this version needs the database change already made on your Supabase project.
    Upload this folder to Netlify again (Deploys, then drag the folder in).

ALSO NEW: CHOOSE WHAT TO TEACH (DISCIPLE SERIES OR OHB SERIES)
  * Teachers: open the Class tab. At the top is "Teaching material" with both series. Tap "Teach this" twice
    to switch. The class restarts at Lesson 1 and check-in closes. A new teacher can also pick the material
    when they start a class.
  * Each student's progress, journal, verses and attendance are kept separately for each material.
    Switching back to a material brings everything back exactly as it was.
  * OHB Series: 48 lessons in four series (Salvation 1 to 12, Maturity 13 to 24, Discipleship 25 to 36,
    Victorious Life 37 to 48). The teaching text keeps the OHB wording. The discussion questions come from
    the PDFs' application questions. The weekly tasks, key question and memory verse were added for the app.
    Scripture quotes show the World English Bible, not the NIV used in the PDFs.
  * One update needed: this version needs the database change already made on your Supabase project.
    Nothing to do there. Upload this folder to Netlify again (Deploys, then drag the folder in).

HOW TO ADD ANOTHER MATERIAL LATER (for whoever builds the app)
  1. Put the lessons in a JSON file with the same shape as ohb_lessons.json (numbered 1 to N).
  2. Add an entry to app/materials.json: id, name, off (the next thousand: 2000, 3000), file, desc and phases.
  3. Add one row to the materials table in Supabase (id, label, week_offset equal to off).
  4. Run bible/mkdata.py (add the new file to its list) and app/mksite.py, then upload the site folder.

FOLLOW-UP TOOLS FOR TEACHERS (new)
  * Earlier weeks: on the Today page, under "Who is here today", tap "Take attendance for an earlier week".
    Pick the class, tap the names of those who were there. It saves as you tap. Tap Done when finished.
  * May need a call: on Today, the app lists students who seem to be slipping. That means they have not read
    two of the last three lessons, missed the last two classes, or have not opened the app in two weeks.
    New students are left out for their first one or two weeks. Tap a name to open that student.
  * Private notes: open any student (People tab, tap the name). Write a note, and add a follow-up date if you
    like. Notes are seen by you and co-leaders of the same class only. The student never sees them.
    When a follow-up date arrives, the student appears under "Follow-ups due" on Today until you mark it done.
  * Students see a gentle "Catch up" card on their home page if they missed a recent class or have not read
    a recent lesson. It shows the last five lessons only, so nobody is buried by a long list.
  * Add to Home Screen: students and teachers see a short hint with the steps for their phone. "Not now" hides it.

GOOD TO KNOW
  * Students see their own journal and nobody else's. Your dashboard shows names, lessons finished,
    and only the journal entries a student chooses to share. Prayer requests marked "Only my teacher"
    are visible to you only. Requests shared with the class are visible to classmates.
  * Anyone can make a teacher account and start their own separate class. Classes never see each other.
    Other pastors can use the same link for their own groups.
  * Supabase's free plan pauses a project after about a week with no activity. The keep-alive step above
    prevents this. If the app ever says it cannot reach the server, open the dashboard link above and tap Restore.
  * To use a different database later, edit config.js (two values) and re-upload the folder.
  * The Bible text is the World English Bible (public domain).
