Privacy

Last updated 15 November 2026

Discipleship Class is a small app that a teacher uses to run a discipleship class. This page says what it keeps about you and who can see it.

What the app keeps

  • Your name and email address, and the class you joined.
  • Your progress: lessons finished, verses and tasks ticked, and which classes you attended.
  • Your journal entries.
  • Prayer requests you add.
  • If you turn on reminders, a notification address from your device so the app can send you class notices.

Who can see it

  • Your teacher sees your name, your progress, whether you wrote in your journal, and your attendance.
  • Your journal text stays private unless you choose to share an entry with your teacher.
  • Prayer requests are visible only to the people you choose when you write them: your teacher only, or the group.
  • Your teacher may keep private notes about you for follow-up. Only teachers can see those.
  • Nothing is sold, and nothing is used for advertising.

Where it is kept

Your data is stored with Supabase, the database and sign-in service the app uses. The app itself is hosted on GitHub Pages. Lessons are saved on your device so the app opens when your connection is poor.

Deleting your account

Students can delete their own account in the app: open Me, then tap Delete my account and confirm. This permanently removes your profile, progress, journal, prayer requests, attendance and any teacher notes about you. It cannot be undone.

Teachers cannot delete their account in the app, because it would remove the whole class. Ask the person who runs the app.

Questions

Ask your teacher. They can reach the person who runs the app.
